# Social media plan (for launch on both stores)

Saved Nov 26, 2026, to use once the iPhone app is in the App Store, so Altiro launches on Google Play and the
App Store together.

## 1. Where to post
- **Instagram**: the main one. Fitness people live there, and Altiro's share cards are already sized for
  Instagram posts and stories.
- **TikTok**: the biggest chance to reach new people with short videos.
- **Strava club, "Altiro"**: free, and reaches exactly the runners and hikers who connect Strava.
- Later: **YouTube Shorts**, reusing the TikTok videos.

Claim the same handle everywhere now, even unused ones, matching the website: **@getaltiro**.

## 2. Tools

| What | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Design posts and graphics | Canva (Brand Kit with Altiro's colors, logo and fonts) | Free; Pro about $15/mo |
| Edit short videos | CapCut | Free |
| Schedule Instagram + Facebook | Meta Business Suite | Free |
| Schedule everything in one place | Buffer (free plan: 3 accounts) | Free; about $6/mo per account after |
| Track what works | Each app's built-in insights | Free |

Starting set: **Canva + CapCut + Buffer free plan**.

## 3. Weekly rhythm (3-4 posts a week)
Rotate four kinds of posts:
1. **Real workouts**: share cards (e.g. the Cucamonga Peak hike), and testers' with their permission.
2. **Quick tips**: pairing running and lifting, deload weeks, treadmill vs. outdoor.
3. **App features**: a 15-30 second screen recording of one feature (indoor tracking, the share card, the
   Strava route).
4. **Behind the scenes**: building the app, tester feedback, the road to the App Store.

About **one hour a week** to make posts and schedule them in Buffer, plus **10 minutes a day** replying to comments.
