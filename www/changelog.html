What's new in Altiro

Updates to the app and website, newest first. Last updated November 28, 2026.

November 2026

  • Nov 28: Website: a clearer welcome page with prices, common questions and an iPhone list; this changelog and a hybrid training guide.
  • Nov 27: Altiro is on Google Play. The website links to the Android app.

  • Nov 24: Strava routes and elevation: runs from your watch show their full route and height line.

  • Nov 14: Best efforts from Strava: your fastest mile and 5K from a watch run.
  • Nov 4: Share card: the route and elevation of a GPS run, with every split.

October 2026

  • Oct 20: Photo demos for dozens of strength exercises.
  • Oct 13: Runs and steps from Apple Health and Health Connect.
  • Oct 5: Workout reminders in the phone app; recorded voice cues on the interval timer.

September 2026
